CareerAiHub: AI career tools for job seekers

Check your resume by fixed rules and see what to fix first, then practise interviews and salary talks and plan your next step. Feedback shows its evidence. The default market is Singapore.

What you can do

  • ATS Scanner: check a resume against a job description
  • Resume Builder and Cover Letter AI
  • Interview Coach and Salary Prep
  • Skills Gap and Career Roadmap
  • Verify Creds (in development): check certificates from allowlisted issuers
  • Job Search and AI Career Coach

FAQ

What is CareerAiHub? A web app with AI career tools for job seekers: resume checker and builder, cover letter, interview and salary practice, skills gap and career roadmap. Credential checks are in development. An early employer portal exists, and candidate matching is planned.

Which country is it for? The default market in the app is Singapore.

Do I need an account? Job search works without signing in. Sign in to save your data and use the AI tools.
