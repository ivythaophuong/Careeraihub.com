CareerAiHub: AI career tools for job seekers

Scan your resume like a senior hiring manager, see what is costing you interviews, then practise interviews and salary talks. Verified skills, AI coaching and smarter matching. The default market is Singapore.

What you can do

  • ATS Scanner: check a resume against a job description
  • Resume Builder and Cover Letter AI
  • Interview Coach and Salary Prep
  • Skills Gap and Career Roadmap
  • Verify Creds: check certificates from allowlisted issuers
  • Job Search and AI Career Coach

FAQ

What is CareerAiHub? A web app with AI career tools for job seekers: resume scan and builder, cover letter, interview and salary practice, skills gap and credential checks. It also has a portal for employers.

Which country is it for? The default market in the app is Singapore.

Do I need an account? Job search works without signing in. Sign in to save your data and use the AI tools.
